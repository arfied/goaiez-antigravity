**BAA between Customer ("Covered Entity" or upstream business associate) and [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("Business Associate")** — required before any PHI use of the Services. ⚖️ NOTE: healthcare counsel review is an existing prelaunch gate; this draft is the working baseline.

**1. Definitions.** Terms have the meanings in HIPAA (45 CFR Parts 160, 164): PHI, ePHI, Breach, Security Incident, Required by Law, etc. "PHI" here means PHI Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits for Covered Entity via the Services.

**2. Permitted Uses & Disclosures.** BA may use/disclose PHI only: (a) to perform the Services under the Agreement; (b) as Required by Law; (c) for BA's proper management and administration and legal responsibilities, with disclosures only if Required by Law or with reasonable assurances of confidentiality and breach notice from the recipient; (d) for Data Aggregation services relating to Covered Entity's health care operations; and (e) to **de-identify PHI per §164.514(b)**, after which the data is not PHI and may be used per the ToS. BA will not use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate Subpart E if done by Covered Entity, follows Covered Entity's notice of privacy practices restrictions communicated to BA, and applies **Minimum Necessary**.

**3. Safeguards.** BA implements administrative, physical, and technical safeguards per the Security Rule for ePHI, including the platform's PHI partitioning, dedicated key management, access logging of PHI access, and offshore-access exclusion by default.

**4. Reporting.** BA reports to Covered Entity: (a) any use/disclosure not permitted, and any **Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and within [30] days** of discovery, with §164.410 content; (b) Security Incidents — with **unsuccessful** routine incidents (pings, port scans, blocked malware) reported via this standing notice; (c) any subcontractor breach affecting Covered Entity's PHI.

**5. Subcontractors.** BA ensures subcontractors that create/receive/maintain/transmit PHI agree in writing to restrictions at least as protective as this BAA.

**6. Individual Rights.** Within [15] days of request, BA will make PHI in a Designated Record Set available for access (§164.524) and amendment (§164.526) and incorporate amendments as directed; BA maintains and provides accounting-of-disclosures information (§164.528) within [30] days. Requests received directly from individuals are forwarded to Covered Entity within [5] business days.

**7. HHS Access.** BA makes internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary for compliance determination.

**8. Term & Termination.** This BAA runs with the Agreement. Covered Entity may terminate for material breach uncured within [30] days. On termination, BA **returns or destroys all PHI** (including subcontractor copies) within [30] days; if infeasible, protections extend for as long as PHI is retained and uses are limited to those making return infeasible, documented to Covered Entity.

**9. Miscellaneous.** No third-party beneficiaries; interpretation to permit HIPAA compliance; amendment as needed for legal changes; this BAA controls over the Agreement for PHI; state-law stricter provisions apply where not preempted. Covered Entity will not agree to individual restrictions materially impairing the Services without notice, and will obtain any authorizations needed for the configured features.
